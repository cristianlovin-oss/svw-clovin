# Summit Trail Outfitters – Customer FAQ and Policies

Summit Trail Outfitters is an online-only retailer of outdoor gear (tents, backpacks, sleeping
bags, stoves, clothing and footwear). We ship within the United States only and have no retail stores.

## 1. Returns
- Unused items in original condition with tags attached can be returned within **60 days of delivery**.
- Items marked **"Final Sale"** (clearance), **gift cards**, and **opened fuel canisters** cannot be returned.
- Returns are started online at summittrail.example/returns; a prepaid return label is emailed to the customer.
- Return shipping is **free for exchanges and for defective or wrong items**. For other refunds, a
  **$7.95 return-label fee** is deducted from the refund. **Summit Club members get free returns.**
- Used items cannot be returned after 60 days. Items returned after 60 days are not accepted.

## 2. Refunds
- Refunds go to the **original payment method within 5–7 business days** after our warehouse receives the return.
- Customers can choose **store credit instead and receive a 10% bonus** on the store credit amount.
- Original shipping charges are not refunded, unless the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.

## 3. Shipping
- **Free standard shipping on orders of $75 or more** (before tax) to the contiguous United States.
  Orders under $75 pay **$6.95** for standard shipping.
- Standard shipping takes **3–5 business days**. Expedited (2 business days) costs **$14.95**.
  Overnight costs **$29.95** and must be ordered by **1:00 PM Mountain Time** on a business day.
- Alaska and Hawaii: available with a **$15 surcharge**; overnight is not available.
- Overnight shipping is not available to P.O. boxes.
- We do **not ship internationally**.

## 4. Order changes and cancellations
- Orders can be changed or cancelled within **1 hour** of being placed, using the link in the order
  confirmation email or by contacting support.
- After 1 hour the order is sent to the warehouse and cannot be changed or cancelled; the customer can
  return the items after delivery under the Returns policy.

## 5. Price match
- We match a lower price from an **authorized US retailer** on an **identical, in-stock item** within
  **14 days of purchase** (or before purchase).
- Not eligible: marketplace sellers (for example third-party sellers on Amazon, eBay or Walmart Marketplace),
  clearance or Final Sale items, bundles, coupons or member-only prices, and auction sites.
- Request a price match through live chat or by phone with a link to the competitor's listing.

## 6. Warranty
- **Summit-brand gear**: 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects from the delivery date.
- **Summit-brand backpacks**: **lifetime warranty** against manufacturing defects.
- Other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty; we help customers contact the manufacturer.
- Warranties do not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, accidents, or modifications.
- Warranty claims: email photos of the defect and the order number to warranty@summittrail.example.

## 7. Damaged, defective or wrong items
- Report damaged, defective or wrong items within **7 days of delivery**, with photos, through the
  returns page or by contacting support.
- We ship a **replacement free of charge** (or refund if out of stock) and provide a free return label.

## 8. Summit Club (loyalty program)
- Free to join. Members earn **1 point per $1** spent (before tax and shipping).
- **100 points = $5 reward**, applied at checkout.
- Points expire after **12 months without account activity** (a purchase or return).
- Member benefits: free returns and early access to sales.

## 9. Payment
- Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Summit Trail gift cards.
- **Klarna** pay-over-time is available for orders between **$50 and $1,000**.
- Gift cards **never expire** and cannot be redeemed for cash.

## 10. Privacy and account security
- Customer service will **never ask for a full card number, CVV, or password**.
- Customer service agents (including this assistant) **cannot look up, change, or discuss specific orders
  or accounts**. Order-specific help requires a human agent and the order number plus the email used to order.
- We never share one customer's information with another person.
