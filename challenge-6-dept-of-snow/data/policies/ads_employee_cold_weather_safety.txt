ALASKA DEPARTMENT OF SNOW (ADS) - EMPLOYEE COLD WEATHER AND FATIGUE SAFETY
Policy number: ADS-SAF-400 | Revision 3 | Effective 1 October 2026
Owner: Safety Officer | Fictional document created for training purposes.

1. HOURS OF SERVICE
  - Maximum 16 hours of equipment operation in any 24-hour period.
  - Minimum 8 consecutive hours off duty between shifts.
  - A 30-minute break is required after every 6 hours of plowing.

2. COLD EXPOSURE
  - When the wind chill is -20 F (-29 C) or colder, outdoor manual work (shoveling, chaining) is limited
    to 30 minutes followed by a warm-up break of at least 10 minutes.
  - When the wind chill is -40 F (-40 C) or colder, only essential outdoor work is allowed, and always
    in pairs.
  - Every ADS vehicle carries a cold-weather survival kit (blanket, food, water, flares, first aid).

3. EQUIPMENT CHECKS
  - Pre-trip inspection before every shift: lights, plow hydraulics, spreader, tire chains, radio.
  - A unit with a failed safety item is taken out of service until repaired.

4. INCIDENT REPORTING
  - Any crash, injury or near miss involving ADS equipment is reported to the supervisor immediately
    and in writing within 24 hours.
