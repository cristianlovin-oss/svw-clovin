ALASKA DEPARTMENT OF SNOW (ADS) - PUBLIC SERVICE REQUESTS POLICY
Policy number: ADS-PUB-300 | Revision 2 | Effective 1 October 2026
Owner: Customer Service Division | Fictional document created for training purposes.

1. HOW THE PUBLIC CAN REPORT PROBLEMS
  - Online form at ads.alaska.example/report, the ADS mobile app, or phone 1-907-555-0199 (24/7 during
    declared storms; 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM otherwise).
  - Emergencies (crashes, stranded or injured people) must be reported to 911, not to ADS.

2. REQUEST TYPES AND TARGET RESPONSE TIMES
  - Unplowed or missed road: reviewed within 4 hours during a storm; scheduled according to road
    priority (ADS-OPS-100).
  - Icy road or intersection needing sand: P1 within 2 hours, P2 within 6 hours, P3 within 24 hours.
  - Blocked storm drain or flooding from melt: within 24 hours.
  - Damaged mailbox caused by a plow: inspected within 5 business days.
  - Damaged property (fence, landscaping, vehicle) caused by ADS equipment: claim reviewed within
    30 days; claims must be filed within 60 days of the damage.
  - Snow berm assistance (Senior and Disability Berm Assistance program): berm cleared within
    24 hours after the street is plowed. Residents must register by 1 November each year; residents
    must be 65 or older, or have a documented disability, and have no able-bodied adult in the household.

3. MAILBOX DAMAGE
  - ADS repairs or reimburses up to $75 for a standard mailbox, or $150 for a mailbox and post,
    only when the mailbox was installed to USPS standards and was physically hit by ADS equipment.
  - Damage from the weight of snow thrown by the plow is not covered.

4. WHAT ADS DOES NOT DO
  - Plow private driveways, private roads or parking lots.
  - Remove snow from sidewalks in front of private property (the property owner's responsibility
    under most municipal codes).
  - Give legal, medical or insurance advice.

5. PRIVACY
  - Service request records contain the requester's name and contact details. Staff and automated
    assistants may share the status of a request only with the requester, using the request ID.
