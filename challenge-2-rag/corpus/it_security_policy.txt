LUMORA ROBOTICS - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY (INTERNAL)
Policy ID: SEC-001 - Version 4.0 - Approved by the CISO on 10 January 2026

1. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, contractors and interns who use Lumora systems, devices or data.

2. ACCOUNTS AND PASSWORDS
- Minimum password length: 14 characters. Passphrases are encouraged.
- Passwords must not be reused across systems and must be stored only in the company password manager ("VaultBox").
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all accounts. Hardware security keys are mandatory for administrators and for anyone with production access.
- Accounts are locked after 8 failed login attempts.

3. DEVICES
- All laptops must use full-disk encryption and the managed endpoint protection agent.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Personal devices may access email and chat only through the managed mobile app, never through a browser.
- USB storage devices are blocked by default. Exceptions require an IT ticket and CISO approval.

4. NETWORK ACCESS
- Remote access to internal systems requires the "LumoConnect" VPN.
- Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN switched on.
- Robot fleet networks at customer sites are isolated and must never be connected to the corporate network.

5. DATA CLASSIFICATION
- Public: marketing material, published datasheets.
- Internal: handbooks, internal processes (this document).
- Confidential: pricing, customer contracts, roadmaps, source code.
- Restricted: personal data, security keys, customer site maps.
Confidential and Restricted data must not be pasted into external AI tools unless the tool is on the approved list maintained by IT.

6. INCIDENT REPORTING
- Report any suspected security incident (phishing, lost device, malware, data leak) within 1 hour of discovery.
- Report via the #security-incidents chat channel or email security@lumora.example. Outside business hours, call the on-call security line at extension 4444.
- Lost or stolen devices must also be reported to IT so they can be wiped remotely.

7. TRAINING
- Security awareness training is mandatory within the first 2 weeks of employment and then once every year.
- Phishing simulations run every quarter. Employees who click a simulated phishing link must complete a 15-minute refresher module.
