LUMORA ROBOTICS - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (INTERNAL)
Version 7.2 - Effective 1 March 2026
Owner: People & Culture team (people@lumora.example)

1. ABOUT LUMORA ROBOTICS
Lumora Robotics GmbH designs and manufactures autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for warehouses and hospitals. Headquarters: Munich, Germany. Engineering office: Cluj-Napoca, Romania. Sales office: Rotterdam, Netherlands. Employee count (January 2026): 412.

2. WORKING HOURS AND HYBRID WORK
- Standard working week: 38 hours.
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Thursday. Fridays have no core hours ("Focus Friday").
- Hybrid policy: employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Team "anchor day" in the office is Tuesday for all teams.
- Fully remote work requires written approval from the department head and the People & Culture team.
- Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year, and requires a request in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.

3. PAID TIME OFF
- Annual leave: 28 working days per year for full-time employees (pro-rated for part-time).
- Additional leave: +1 day after 3 years of service, +2 days after 5 years of service.
- Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.
- Sick leave: notify your manager before 09:30 on the first day. A doctor's certificate is required from the 3rd consecutive day of sickness.
- Volunteer day: every employee gets 1 paid volunteer day per year.

4. PARENTAL LEAVE
- Primary caregivers: 20 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Secondary caregivers: 6 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption.
- Lumora tops up statutory parental benefits to 100% of base salary during these periods.

5. EXPENSES AND TRAVEL
- Submit expenses in the "Spendly" tool within 30 days of the expense date.
- Meal allowance on business travel: up to EUR 45 per day.
- Hotel cap: EUR 160 per night (EUR 220 in London, Paris and Zurich).
- Flights under 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy.
- Trains are preferred over flights for trips under 4 hours.
- Any single expense above EUR 500 requires pre-approval from your manager.

6. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
- Each employee has a learning budget of EUR 1,500 per year for courses, conferences and certifications.
- Up to 5 paid learning days per year.

7. EQUIPMENT
- Engineers receive a laptop with 64 GB RAM; other roles receive 32 GB RAM.
- Home-office allowance: one-time EUR 600, plus EUR 30 per month for internet.
- Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
